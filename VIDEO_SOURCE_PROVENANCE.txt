VIDEO SOURCE AND DELIVERED SCOPE
Updated 15 September 2026.

Dataset: Tilapia-RAS Dataset: Underwater Videos and Polygon-Annotated Frames
with Physicochemical Metadata.
Source: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17518786
Paper: https://doi.org/10.3390/data10120211
Licence: CC BY 4.0, recorded from the paper's Data Availability Statement.

Selected source: GX020206, 720p sample.
Window: source frames 350-649 inclusive, exported as task frames 0-299.
Resolution: 720 x 630. Frame rate: 29.97 fps. Duration: 10.01 seconds.
The window contains a readable primary fish, crossings and partial occlusion.
A full disappearance and re-identification was not demonstrated.

The source authors' polygon annotations were not used to create the tracks.
No comparison against those annotations is claimed.

Delivered 8 September 2026: 6 tracks, 762 visible boxes and 3 outside terminators.
CVAT XML and Datumaro agree on the 762 visible annotations. Twenty logged
attribute ranges account for all 2,286 visible-box attribute values.
Manual placement was reported. Interpolation was not exercised and annotation
working time was not recorded. Zero final-review corrections is a reported
review result, not an independently established error rate.

Consumer notes:
- CVAT's standard occluded flag remains 0. Use the visibility attribute:
  162 full, 387 partial, 213 heavy_occlusion visible boxes.
- context_object is declared but unused in this window.
- A geometry change alone cannot prove an identity error. Review it against
  the frame, visibility and exit flags.

The source clip and native exports remain private. This repository publishes
the method, label schema and checked summary.
